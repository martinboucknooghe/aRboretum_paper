%	Abstract
\Abstract{
  As biodiversity continues to decline at an alarming rate, raising
  public awareness has become a crucial seed from which individual
  consciousness can grow and collective action can flourish. This
  internship report presents aRboretum, an open-source R package
  designed to transform botanical garden labels into effective
  scientific outreach tools. In response to the limitations of
  traditional plant labels and the urgent need for new approaches to
  public engagement, aRboretum automates the production, digitization,
  and enrichment of multilingual botanical labels. It integrates
  reliable taxonomic data, generates descriptions in multiple languages,
  and incorporates both audio and visual features. Designed as a modular
  tool, aRboretum streamlines label production, improves accessibility,
  and promotes inclusion by providing a participatory scientific
  communication platform adaptable to botanical gardens, education,
  biodiversity conservation, and many other applications.
}

\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}

Botanical gardens, such as the Jardim Botânico do Rio de Janeiro (JBRJ),
founded in 1808 and a UNESCO World Heritage Site (UNESCO Heritage
Centre), play an essential multifunctional role for ex situ
conservation, environmental education and taxonomic research. According
to the International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN), these
institutions maintain and house more than 100,000 plant species, or
about 30\% of the world's vascular flora with an overrepresentation of
endangered species (Mounce et al. 2017). The JBRJ participates in this
effort by sheltering more than 8,000 species, many of which are endemic
to Brazil (JBRJ), making it a vital reserve of native flora for unique
ecosystems such as the Mata Atlântica, the Cerrado or the Amazon.
